Small network.
Open notebook.

42 signed board inputs, 64 ReLU units, seven policy logits and a tanh value head. 3,272 parameters. Inputs are from the side-to-move's perspective; value signs alternate on backup. No expert games or positional evaluation features.

Edition II trains from the same seed 311 for a fixed 1,200 self-play games at 64 simulations per move. Every 20 games: 160 AdamW updates, learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.0001, batch up to 128, gradient clip 5. Replay holds 24,000 positions; horizontal reflection augmentation. Training targets are root visit proportions and terminal outcomes.

Root Dirichlet noise α 0.3, fraction 0.25. Sample visits for the first ten plies, greedy afterward. Policy cross-entropy plus value MSE. This is an AlphaZero-style educational loop, not a reproduction of AlphaZero's architecture, scale or performance. The exact solver is not used for learning.
